# ADR 005 — Synthetic demo

Decision: 20 known synthetic products over 730 days, seed 42, fixed 2025-12-31 cutoff,
GBP and Europe/London. Include intermittent products and simulated operations.
Reason: a reproducible offline demo without a paid key or large external download.
Tradeoff: synthetic data cannot substantiate real-world business impact or generalization.
